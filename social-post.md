Customers shouldn’t have to repeat the troubleshooting that already failed.

I built RecallDesk: a support workspace where Hindsight retains outcome-aware customer memory. When a customer returns, the agent recalls what failed, what worked, and how they prefer to be helped—then changes its response accordingly.

The demo follows a CloudSync sync issue: no more suggesting reinstall and cache clearing after those already failed. A network fix is reused, new Wi-Fi context is retained, and the next ticket benefits too.
