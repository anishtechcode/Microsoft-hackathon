# RecallDesk pitch outline

1. RecallDesk — customer support that remembers what worked.
2. The problem — ticket history loses outcome knowledge.
3. Why history isn’t enough — failed steps get repeated.
4. The solution — outcome-aware, customer-scoped memory.
5. Hindsight lifecycle — Recall → decide → resolve → retain.
6. Architecture — browser, agent endpoint, Hindsight bank, knowledge context.
7. Aarav live demo — a repeat sync issue.
8. Before / after — generic support versus memory-informed support.
9. Impact — continuity and less repeated troubleshooting.
10. Final takeaway — remember what worked.
